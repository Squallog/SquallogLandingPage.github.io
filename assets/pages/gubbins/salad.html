Chicken Salad

Ingredients.
  • Chicken breast - 2 fillets.
  • Avocado - 2.
  • Medium Carrot.
  • Rocket - Half a bag.
  • Cucumber - Half
  • Salt - 1 tablespoon.
  • Lemon - 1
  • Olive Oil - 2 Tablespoons
  • Basil - 3 Fresh Leaves
  • Garlic - 2 cloves
  • Ground black pepper - 1 tablespoon
  • Oregano - 1 tablespoon
  • Parmasan cheese - 10g

Procedure.

Juice the Lemons into a selable tupperware (other containers are available).
Set the lenon caracass asside for later use.

Crush both cloves of Garlic, add them to the container along with the 2 tablespoons of Olive Oil, Oregano, and Black Pepper. Mix together inside the container.

Dice the Chicken Fillets into bite sized chunks, put these inside the container.

Add the Lemon carcasses and fresh Basil leaves into the container, seal the container and shake until the chicken is thouroughly covered in marinade.

Place the container in a fridge and leave to marinate for at least 8 hours.
