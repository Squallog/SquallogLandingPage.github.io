Chili

it's just chili.

Ingredients
  • Sweetcorn - 150g
  • Black Beans - 150g
  • Black Eyed Peas - 150g
  • Minced Meat (pork, beef, or mix) - 150g
  • Jalapenos - 30g
  • Onion - 1 large or 2 medium
  • Limes - 2
  • Fresh Coriander - 1 average 30 year old male handful
  • Ground cumin seeds - 2 tablespoons
  • Oregano - 2 tablespoons
  • Onion powder - 2 tablespoons
  • Salt - 1 tablespoon
  • Smoked Paprika - 1 tablespoon
  • Turmeric - 1 tablespoon
  • Vegetable Oil - 3 tablespoons
  • Vegetable Stock - 250ml

Procedure
  • Into a large frying pan pour 26g vegetable oil. Apply high heat until shimmering
  • Pour Vegetable Stock into a large saucepan. Apply heat to bring to a simmer.
  • Drain Sweetcorn if inside can with water, pour into pan along with 4g salt and 8g smoked paprika. Fry until lightly charred, stirring regularly, then transfer into suacepan with stock.
  • Drain Black Beans if inside cn with water, add to the pan along with ground cumin seeds. Fry until shrivelled, stirring regularly, then transfer into suacepan with stock.
  • Dice Onion(s) into fine chunks, reduce heat to medium before adding Onion chunks to pan. Add half of the Turmeric and fry until soft and golden, adding remaining oil as needed, then transfer into suacepan with stock.
  • Chop Limes into quarters, squeeze juice into a glass. Add Mince into pan, along with remaining Turmeric, onion powder, oregano, and remaining salt. Pour lime juice into pan and stir thoroughly. Fry until meat is browned before transferring into the saucepan with stock.
  • Dice Jalapenos, add into the pan along with Black Eyed Peas and stir thoroughly. Fry until beans softened before transfering into saucepan with stock.
  • Allow everything to simmer in the saucepan for at least 10 minutes before preparing to serve.
  • Dice Coriander, bring the pan off the heat before adding the diced coriander, stir thoroughly.

Serve with rice and soured cream, or in wrap.
